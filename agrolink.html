More than a product listing site

AgroLink is designed to make agricultural trading more organised, from expected harvest to a completed delivery.

🌱

Expected harvests

Farmers can record anticipated harvest dates and quantities so buyers can plan procurement earlier.

🔗

Supply aggregation

Help identify multiple suppliers whose combined quantities may satisfy a larger buyer request.

🎯

Demand matching

Compare product, volume, location and timing to identify potential matches—not just keyword search.

📊

Market intelligence

Future versions can use verified listings and transactions to show seasonal and regional patterns.

🧾

Transaction records

Prepare a record of order terms, quantity, delivery and status to improve coordination and accountability.

🚚

Delivery coordination

Help buyers and sellers plan collection and transport with independent logistics providers.
